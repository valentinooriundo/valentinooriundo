## About me

I'm a **Systems Engineer** focused on **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence**, with a growing interest in **Data Science and Machine Learning**.

I enjoy taking a business question, working through the data, finding what actually matters, and presenting the result in a way that is easy to understand.

Most of my work revolves around **SQL, Power BI, Python and Excel**. I'm also strengthening my skills in statistics, exploratory data analysis and machine learning.

> I care about useful analysis, clear communication and projects that solve real problems.
